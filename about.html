But when local owners went shopping for it, they found agencies quoting agency prices: thousands up front, long contracts, and a salesperson who disappears after the signature.

A.I. NOW was built to be the other option. Same technology. Same work. Priced at 42% of what typical agencies charge, printed on this website for anyone to read, month-to-month, with a free audit before you spend a single dollar.

How we do business

Four rules run this company. They're also the reason our website looks the way it does — plain, priced, and in writing.

💲 42% pricing — no agency bloat
A traditional agency's price includes account managers, sales commissions, office rent, and layers of overhead — before a single line of your project gets built. We run lean on purpose: no sales team, no bloat, and AI itself does much of the heavy lifting in our own operation. That savings is the business model, and it goes to you: every price here is set at 42% of what typical agencies charge, and published openly instead of hidden behind a "book a discovery call" wall.
📞 A real person answers
We sell AI, but we don't hide behind it. When you call (586) 200-8282 or write to support@artificial-intelligencenow.com, you reach a real person who knows your account — the same way your customers should reach a real business when it matters. AI does the heavy lifting: the instant answers, the 2 AM lead captures, the follow-ups that never get forgotten. People do the judgment, the setup, and the accountability.
🔓 Month-to-month, always
No long contracts and no cancellation fees. After your setup, every plan is month-to-month: cancel anytime and the service runs to the end of the month you've paid for. If our work stops earning its keep, you should be free to walk — we'd rather earn the renewal every month than lock you into one.
🤝 Straight answers, even when it costs us
AI will not guarantee you more sales, and we won't pretend otherwise. It answers faster, misses fewer inquiries, and follows up every time — what you close is still up to you. If AI isn't a good fit for your business, the free audit will say so. We'd rather lose a sale than sell you something that won't pay for itself.
