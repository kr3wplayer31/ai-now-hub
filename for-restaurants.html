The daily reality in restaurants

Your team is hired to cook, serve and host. The phone and the inbox don't care — they interrupt all night, and the questions they carry are almost always the same ones.

📞 The phone rings during the rush
A host pulled off the floor mid-service to answer "what time do you close?" is a guest at a table waiting longer. Most of those calls are questions your website could have answered in seconds.
📥 Catering inquiries sit in an inbox
Catering and large-party requests are some of the best money in this business — and they usually land in an email inbox and wait a day or two for a reply. By then, the customer has often booked somewhere else.
🕘 Menu, hours, "do you take reservations?"
Guests decide where to eat from their phones, often an hour before they walk in. If your site makes them call for basic answers, plenty won't — they'll pick the place that answered on the page.
⭐ Your reviews are your reputation
The great reviews deserve a fast, gracious reply; the rough ones deserve one even more. Together they're the first impression for every guest who finds you on Google — and most restaurants never answer any of them.
