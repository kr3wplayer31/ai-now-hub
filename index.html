📋 The AI Starter Prompt Pack — copy-ready prompts that actually work

Hit Copy on any prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI chat. Swap the [brackets] for your details.

Explain anything, plainly

Explain [topic] to me like I'm smart but brand new to it. Use one real-world example, skip the jargon, and finish with the 3 things that matter most.

Fix my email

Rewrite the email below so it's shorter, friendly and clear. Keep every fact exactly as-is. Give me two versions: polite and direct. [PASTE YOUR EMAIL HERE]

Summarize anything long

Summarize the text below in 5 bullet points a busy person can read in 30 seconds. Then add one line: "The one thing to remember is…" [PASTE THE ARTICLE OR DOCUMENT HERE]

Write a social post that sounds like me

Write 3 short social media captions about [what you're posting]. Casual and human, no hype words, no fake urgency. One funny, one helpful, one straight to the point.

An honest cover letter

Write a cover letter for the [job title] job at [company] using ONLY the real experience I paste below. Do not invent employers, dates or duties. Keep it under 250 words and confident. [PASTE YOUR EXPERIENCE HERE]

Test a small-business idea

I'm thinking about starting [business idea] in [city]. Play devil's advocate: give me the 5 hardest questions a customer or a bank would ask, what it realistically costs to start, and the cheapest way to test it this month before spending real money.

Learn anything in a week

Build me a 7-day starter plan to learn [skill]. One focused lesson per day, each under an hour, each ending with a tiny hands-on task. Assume I know nothing on day 1.

Meal plan on a budget

Make me a 5-day dinner plan for [number] people with a grocery budget of $[amount]. Simple ingredients I can find at any grocery store, nothing fancy, and give me the shopping list sorted by aisle.

Turn notes into a to-do list

Turn these rough notes into a clean action list. Group them by what I can do today, this week, and later. Flag anything that sounds like it has a deadline. [PASTE YOUR NOTES HERE]

Brainstorm, then narrow it down

Give me 10 ideas for [goal — e.g. posts for my shop, gifts for my mom, side income this month]. Then rank your own list: which 3 are best for someone with almost no budget, and why.
