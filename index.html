Straight answers

The questions every business owner asks us first — answered the same way we'd answer across a desk.

Will this guarantee me more sales or customers?

No — and be careful with anyone who promises that. What AI does is mechanical: it answers faster, misses fewer inquiries, and follows up every time. Businesses lose work every week to slow responses and unanswered questions; this closes those gaps. What you close is still up to you and your crew.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans are month-to-month. Cancel whenever you like and the service keeps running to the end of the month you've already paid for — no cancellation fees and no hard feelings. Setup fees are non-refundable once work has begun, because that work is custom-built for your business.

Do I own my website, my content and my customer data?

Yes. Your website, your content and your customer information belong to you. We configure AI tools for your business; we don't claim ownership of your site or your data, and we never sell personal data. See our Privacy Policy.

Can the AI make mistakes?

Yes — AI can occasionally get something wrong, which is why we train it on your actual business information, set clear boundaries on what it should and shouldn't answer, and test it with you before launch. You're responsible for reviewing customer-facing outputs, and we'll adjust the AI whenever you spot something you'd phrase differently.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your website address (or your current site login, if we need to install code), your services, hours, service area and pricing basics — the same things your customers ask about. The audit call covers all of it; most owners spend about 30 minutes total on their end.

How fast can this go live?

Most chatbot installs go live within one to two weeks of us receiving your information and site access. Voice receptionist and full website builds take longer. We'll give you a realistic timeline in your written quote — and we'd rather tell you straight than promise a date we can't keep.

I don't have a website yet. Can you still help?

Yes — that's exactly what the AI-Ready Website Build is for: a new website with the chatbot and lead capture built in from day one, starting at $1,499 plus a hosting plan.

Why are the prices "starting at"?

Because a one-page site for a barber and a multi-location dental practice aren't the same job. The starting price is the floor for a standard install; if your project needs more, you get the exact total in writing before we begin. The quoted price is the price you pay — no surprise add-ons mid-project.
