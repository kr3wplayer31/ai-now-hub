Why businesses go private

Cloud AI is powerful — but every question your team asks it, and every document it touches, leaves your building. A private AI box keeps the assistant, and the data, inside your walls.

⚖️ Law offices
Client files, case notes, and contracts are privileged. An assistant that reads them has to live where they live — in your office, under your control — not on a cloud server shared with the world.
🩺 Medical & dental
Patient information carries legal duties. A local AI assistant can help your front desk with policies, forms, and scheduling information without patient details ever leaving the practice.
🏭 Factories & shops
Proprietary drawings, quotes, and process documents are your competitive edge. A private box lets your team search and use them with AI help — with nothing uploaded anywhere.
💼 Finance & professional services
Client financials and internal records stay on your premises, while your team still gets an assistant that knows your documents, templates, and procedures.
🔒 Your data never leaves the building
The assistant runs on the box, in your office. Your documents are loaded onto it by us during setup — they are not sent to a cloud AI service to be answered.
🧾 No per-seat cloud AI bills
One machine, one setup. No stack of per-user monthly AI subscriptions for the whole office — the box serves your team from your own desk.
📶 Works when the internet doesn't
Once it's set up, the assistant itself runs locally — an internet outage doesn't take your office's AI down with it.
