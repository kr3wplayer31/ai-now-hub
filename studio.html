🧭 How it works

Four steps, no surprises. Typical timing: most one-page sites are ready inside a week, and bigger builds are usually two to three weeks — those are honest typical ranges, not guarantees; your quote includes the timeline we commit to for your job.

1
Tell us about the project
Email us what you want built — a few sentences is plenty. What is it, who's it for, and anything you already have (photos, a logo, a page you like the look of).
2
Flat quote + timeline
We look at the job and send one flat price and a delivery timeline, in writing. No hourly billing, no surprise add-ons. If we can't do it well, we'll tell you that instead.
3
We build — you preview
Our AI crew builds it and human eyes check every page. You get a live preview link to click through on your own phone before anything goes public.
4
One revision round, then live
Send one round of changes — wording, photos, colors — and we fold them in. Then it goes live on your domain, and it's yours.

📋 What we need from you

A short list. The clearer your starting material, the faster and better the build.

✍️ Your info & photos
Your business name, what you sell or do, your prices, hours, location and contact details — plus real photos of your work, products or place. Real photos beat stock photos every time, and they're yours to trust.
🔑 Accounts you own
Some builds need accounts that must be in your name: your domain name, your Shopify account for a store, your payment accounts. You open them; we build inside them. Your business stays in your name — that's deliberate.
🛡️ Logins, shared safely
Never email us a password in plain text — not to us, not to anyone. When a build needs access, we arrange a secure handoff (a password manager share or a one-time secure link), and you can revoke it the moment the job is done.
