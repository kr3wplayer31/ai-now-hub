Print questions, answered straight
Do you print everything yourselves?

No — and we won't pretend otherwise. A.I. NOW handles your design, files, quote, proof, and order follow-through. Established print partners produce the work and ship it to you.

Why is pricing quote-based?

Because quantity, size, material, finishing, turnaround, and shipping change the real cost. A written quote means you approve the true total — design fee included when it applies — before anything is made.

What is the design fee?

A separate fee when we create or substantially prepare your artwork. It's quoted and approved before design work begins; if you supply print-ready art, there's no design fee.

When does production begin?

Only after you approve the final proof and the written total. Nothing goes to the printer on a "probably fine."

How long will delivery take?

It depends on the product and the partner, so we confirm the estimated window in writing for your specific order before production.

What if something arrives wrong?

Tell us right away with photos. Reprint or remedy handling follows the producing partner's policy for that product, and we'll pursue it for you — the specifics are confirmed with your order.
