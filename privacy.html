Privacy Policy

A.I. NOW · Last updated November 2026

This policy explains what information A.I. NOW ("we," "us") collects through this website and our AI implementation services, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

1. What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email us, use the live chat on this site, or request a free AI audit, we receive what you provide — typically your name, business name, email address, phone number, website address, and the contents of your message.
  • Client project information. If you become a client, we receive the business information needed to configure your services (services, hours, pricing, service area, policies) and access credentials you choose to share for installation. We never ask for passwords by email.
  • Lead information handled by your AI tools. If we install a chatbot, voice receptionist or follow-up system for your business, that system processes your customers' inquiries (such as names, phone numbers and message contents) on your behalf, so the leads can be delivered to you.
  • Basic site analytics. Like most websites, we may collect standard technical information about visits — pages viewed, approximate location (city/region), device and browser type, and referring site — to understand how the site is used and improve it.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide free audits and quotes.
  • To set up, operate, maintain and support the services you purchase.
  • To deliver leads captured by your AI tools to you.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To send service-related messages about your account or project. We do not send marketing email to people who haven't asked for it.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell personal data — yours or your customers' — to anyone, for any purpose.
  • We never use your customers' lead information for our own marketing.
  • We never share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies: essential cookies that keep the site and the live-chat widget working, and — where analytics are enabled — cookies that help us count visits and understand which pages are useful. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, though the chat widget may not remember an open conversation. Third-party embeds (such as the chat widget) may set their own cookies as described in Section 5.

5. Third-party processors

We use a small set of service providers to run this site and our services. They process information only as needed to provide their function:

  • Live chat — Tawk.to. The chat widget on this site is provided by Tawk.to. If you use it, your messages and basic technical details are processed by Tawk.to so we can receive and reply to them.
  • Hosting and infrastructure. Our website and client AI tools run on third-party hosting and AI platform providers, which process data as part of delivering the service.
  • Email. Email you send us is handled by standard email providers on the way to our inbox.
  • Client platforms. Where your project involves your own accounts (for example, your website host or your Google Business Profile), those platforms process data under their own privacy policies.

6. How long we keep it

Inquiry and audit information is kept as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for the life of the relationship and a reasonable period afterward for records and support. Lead data handled by your AI tools belongs to you and is retained according to your service configuration; ask us if you'd like it exported or deleted.

7. Security

We use reputable providers, account access controls, and encrypted connections (HTTPS) for this website. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information in our care — and we collect as little of it as the job allows.

8. Your choices and requests

You can ask us at any time what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email support@artificial-intelligencenow.com with the subject line PRIVACY and we will respond. If you are a client, your customers' requests about their own lead information can be routed through you or sent to us directly and we'll work with you to honor them.

9. Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "last updated" date above will change.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@artificial-intelligencenow.com. Prefer Gmail? ainowstore@gmail.com. A.I. NOW is based in Detroit, Michigan, USA.
