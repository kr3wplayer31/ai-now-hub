The daily reality in most practices

None of this is your staff's fault. It's what happens when the only way to reach a busy practice is a phone that stops being answered at closing time.

📞 The phone is the only door
Booking runs through the front desk — and when the desk is with a patient, on another line, or gone for the day, the appointment request simply doesn't happen. The patient doesn't leave a message. They call the next practice.
🌙 After-hours questions go unanswered
Hours, insurance, "do you take my plan?", directions, first-visit forms — patients ask these at night and on weekends, from their phones. The practice whose website answers first earns the booking.
🗓️ Closed days still get calls
Plenty of practices close two or three days a week or run half-days. Patients still search, still call, still need appointments on those days — and those requests wait, or wander off to a competitor.
⭐ Reviews sit unanswered
Patients read your Google reviews before they ever call. A thoughtful reply — to the glowing ones and the rough ones — is part of how a practice looks cared-for. Most practices never get to them.
