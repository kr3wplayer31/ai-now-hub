You run your business. We handle the AI.

No software to learn, no dashboard to babysit, no "implementation team" meetings. Five steps, most of them ours — your total time on the whole project is usually about one short call plus a look at the finished product before it goes live.

The five steps

Most installs are live within days of your approval — your exact timeline is confirmed in your written quote, in writing, before any work begins.

1
Free AI Audit
Send us your website address. We look at how customers find you, where inquiries get dropped — the unanswered form, the after-hours visit, the slow reply — and what AI would actually do about it. You get a plain-English report. Free, and no obligation to buy anything.
2
Written Quote
If AI makes sense for your business, you get a written quote with the exact price — setup, monthly, what's included, and your launch timeline. No surprises, no add-ons sprung mid-project. The price we quote is the price you pay. If AI doesn't make sense for you, the audit says that too, and it still cost you nothing.
3
Build & Install
We do it all, on your site. We train the AI on your services, hours, pricing and service area, set its boundaries — what it answers, what it hands to a human — and install it on your existing website. You don't touch code, and your site stays yours.
4
Launch & Train
You see exactly what your customers will see, and we test it together before it goes live — nothing launches until you're happy with it. Then a staff training session shows your team how it works, what it handles, and how leads reach them.
5
Monthly Care
Your monthly plan covers hosting, tuning and updates. Prices change, hours change, services change — tell us and we update the AI. You get a simple report on what your AI handled, and plans are month-to-month: cancel anytime, and the service runs to the end of your paid month.

About timelines — straight

Most standard installs — a chatbot, review responder, lead follow-up — are live within days of your approval and us receiving your information and site access.
