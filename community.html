🤝 House rules — short and human

Four rules, everywhere A.I. NOW people gather. They keep the room worth being in.

1
Be kind
Argue with ideas, not people. Beginners get patience, not pile-ons.
2
Label AI-made work
If AI helped make it, say so. Honest labels are the whole brand here.
3
No spam, no scams
No link-dropping, no miracle-money pitches, no "guaranteed" anything. It gets removed.
4
No advice dressed as fact
Share experience and opinions — but nobody here gives financial or medical advice to other members.
