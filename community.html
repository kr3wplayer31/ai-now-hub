No long-term contract. After setup, every plan is month-to-month. You can cancel anytime — no cancellation fees, no exit interviews, no hard feelings. We'd rather earn your renewal every month than lock you into one. Full details are in our Terms & Conditions.

Will this guarantee me more sales or customers?

No — and be careful with anyone who promises that. What AI does is mechanical: it answers faster, misses fewer inquiries, and follows up every time. Businesses lose work every week to slow responses and unanswered questions; this closes those gaps. What you close is still up to you and your crew. Anyone who guarantees you revenue from a chatbot is selling you something.

What can AI actually do for my business?

The practical jobs: answer customer questions on your website 24/7 (hours, services, pricing, service area), capture leads with names and numbers instead of letting visitors drift away, follow up instantly by text and email, answer your phone and help with bookings, respond to Google reviews, and keep your content and social posts going on schedule. It's a front desk that never sleeps, never calls in sick, and never forgets to follow up.

What can't AI do?

Honest list: it can't do the work itself — it won't fix a roof, cut hair, or treat a patient. It shouldn't give professional advice in regulated fields (no legal advice, no medical diagnoses), and we set those boundaries during setup. It can also occasionally get an answer wrong, which is why we train it on your actual business information and test it with you before launch. Think of it as a very fast, very reliable front desk — not a replacement for you or your crew.

Will it sound robotic?

It sounds like your business, because we train it on your services, your prices, your hours and your way of talking — and you review and approve it before it goes live. If a phrase sounds off, tell us and we adjust it. What it won't do is pretend to be a human being: if a customer needs a real person, the AI hands off and captures their information so you can reach them.

Your website address (and site access if we need to install code — we'll walk you through it), plus the basics your customers ask about: services, hours, service area and pricing. Most owners spend about 30 minutes total on their end, mostly on the audit call. We handle everything else.
