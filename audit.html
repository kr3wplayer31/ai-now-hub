What we check in your free audit

Send us your website address. We go through it the way a customer would — and the way a search engine and an AI assistant would — and check the five places local businesses most often lose work without knowing it.

⏱️ 1. How fast you respond
How long does a new inquiry wait before a human answers? We trace the path a lead takes on your site and flag every spot where the answer is "tomorrow" — because your competitor's answer might be "in two minutes."
🌙 2. After-hours gaps
What happens when a customer finds you at 9 PM, on a Sunday, or on a holiday? We check whether your site can answer questions and capture the lead while you're closed — or whether that visitor simply leaves.
🎯 3. Lead capture
Can a visitor easily leave their name, number and what they need? We look at your forms, your contact options and your follow-up path, and find the leaks where interested customers quietly give up.
⭐ 4. Reviews & reputation
We look at your Google reviews: how many you have, how recent they are, and whether anyone is answering them. Unanswered reviews — especially the rough ones — are silent salespeople working against you.
🔍 5. Search & AI visibility
When someone nearby searches Google — or asks ChatGPT or Gemini — for what you do, can they find you? We check your Google profile and whether your site is set up for AI assistants to read and recommend.
📋 What you receive
A plain-English report covering all five checks: what we found, what it's likely costing you, and exactly which AI services — if any — would fix it, with the published prices attached. If your site is already in good shape, the report says that too. No charge, no obligation, no sales pressure.
