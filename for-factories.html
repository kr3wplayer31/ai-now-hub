The daily reality on the floor

A plant runs on people working with their hands. The phone and the website don't know that — they interrupt all day with questions that are almost always the same ones.

🏭 Supplier and vendor calls hit an empty office
Your office staff are out on the floor — checking a line, walking the warehouse, handling a delivery. Supplier and vendor calls land in voicemail, and the back-and-forth eats the rest of the day.
🧑‍🏭 Applicants ask the same questions all day
"What does it pay? What shifts are open? How do I apply?" Job seekers call with the same three questions, over and over — and every call pulls someone away from the work that actually ships product.
🌙 After-hours inquiries go to voicemail
Customers checking on an order, a buyer asking for a quote, a prospect comparing shops at night — if the only answer after 5 PM is a voicemail box, that inquiry quietly goes to a competitor who answered.
🦺 Routine questions eat office staff time
Shift times, delivery windows, procedure and policy basics, "who do I talk to about…" — your smallest team fields the same questions from employees, vendors and customers, every single day.
🌐 Your website is a phone number and an address
Most plant websites are a logo, a phone number and a map pin. Anyone who finds you online — a buyer, a supplier, a good applicant — has no way to get an answer or leave their details without calling during business hours.
