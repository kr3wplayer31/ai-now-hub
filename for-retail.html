The daily reality for shops, agencies & venues

Three different businesses, one shared leak: people ask buying questions all day — in chat, in comments, in DMs, by email — and every unanswered one is money that quietly walks away.

👕 "Do you have this in a medium?" — asked at midnight
Clothing and retail shoppers ask about sizes, stock, shipping and returns while they're deciding — often late at night, always ready to buy in that moment. If the answer means sending an email and waiting, the moment usually passes.
🎮 Party bookings run through a phone nobody's holding
Gaming lounges and entertainment venues take birthday parties, tournaments and group events — planned by parents and organizers at night, on their phones. Phone-only or form-only booking means those events get planned at the venue that answered.
📱 Agencies: your own site is your loudest portfolio
Social media and creative agencies sell online presence for a living. A prospect who lands on your site and finds no instant answer, no easy next step, notices — it's the one pitch that undercuts itself. Your lead capture should work the way you promise clients theirs will.
💬 Comments and DMs are a sales floor nobody staffs
Buying questions pile up across comments, DMs and inboxes while you're with customers, on a shoot, or running an event. Nobody can watch every channel at once — that's the job AI is actually good at.
