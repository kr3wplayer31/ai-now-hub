The daily reality in the trades

Your hands are your business. That's exactly why the phone is the hardest part of the day to run — and why homeowners end up hiring whoever picked up.

🪜 You're on the job when the phone rings
Quotes and service calls all run through the phone — and when you're on a job site, those calls go to voicemail. Most homeowners don't wait around. They just call the next company on the list.
⛈️ Rushes bury everybody at once
A storm rolls through, the first hot week hits, the first freeze bursts pipes — and every customer calls at the same time. The companies that capture those requests, even roughly, get the work. The rest get silence.
🌙 Quote requests come at night
Homeowners research after work, from the couch. If your site takes quote requests only by phone, or buries them in a form nobody checks till morning, the fastest competitor is already in their inbox.
⭐ Reviews decide who gets called
Before anyone dials, they read your Google reviews. Answered reviews — and a steady flow of new ones from happy customers — are the cheapest advertising a trade company has. Most never get answered at all.
