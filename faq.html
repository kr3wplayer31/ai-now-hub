Money & terms

What it costs, how you're billed, and how you leave if you ever want to.

How much does it cost?

Every price is published on our Pricing page — no "call for a quote" games. As a guide: an AI Website Chatbot is $599 setup + $89/month, packages run from $599 setup + $89/month (Starter) to $1,999 setup + $599/month (All-In Empire), and every price is set at 60% of what typical agencies charge for the same work. Prices are "starting at" because a one-page site for a barber and a multi-location dental practice aren't the same job — your exact total is confirmed in a written quote before any work begins, and the quoted price is the price you pay.

Is there a contract? Am I locked in?

No long-term contract. After setup, every plan is month-to-month. You can cancel anytime — no cancellation fees, no exit interviews, no hard feelings. We'd rather earn your renewal every month than lock you into one. Full details are in our Terms & Conditions.

What happens if I cancel?

Your service keeps running to the end of the month you've already paid for, then it stops. Your website, your content and your customer data remain 100% yours — we remove our AI tools, and your site goes back to exactly what it was. Setup fees are non-refundable once work has begun, because that work is custom-built for your business and can't be resold. There are no other charges for cancelling.

How do I pay?

We accept the major payment methods most businesses already use. See the Get Started page for the current payment options and how billing works for setup fees and monthly plans. You'll always see the exact amount and what it's for before you pay anything.

Will this guarantee me more sales or customers?

No — and be careful with anyone who promises that. What AI does is mechanical: it answers faster, misses fewer inquiries, and follows up every time. Businesses lose work every week to slow responses and unanswered questions; this closes those gaps. What you close is still up to you and your crew. Anyone who guarantees you revenue from a chatbot is selling you something.

The AI itself

What it does, what it doesn't, and whether it will sound like a robot from a phone menu.

What can AI actually do for my business?

The practical jobs: answer customer questions on your website 24/7 (hours, services, pricing, service area), capture leads with names and numbers instead of letting visitors drift away, follow up instantly by text and email, answer your phone and help with bookings, respond to Google reviews, and keep your content and social posts going on schedule. It's a front desk that never sleeps, never calls in sick, and never forgets to follow up.

What can't AI do?

Honest list: it can't do the work itself — it won't fix a roof, cut hair, or treat a patient. It shouldn't give professional advice in regulated fields (no legal advice, no medical diagnoses), and we set those boundaries during setup. It can also occasionally get an answer wrong, which is why we train it on your actual business information and test it with you before launch. Think of it as a very fast, very reliable front desk — not a replacement for you or your crew.

Will it sound robotic?

It sounds like your business, because we train it on your services, your prices, your hours and your way of talking — and you review and approve it before it goes live. If a phrase sounds off, tell us and we adjust it. What it won't do is pretend to be a human being: if a customer needs a real person, the AI hands off and captures their information so you can reach them.

Can the AI make mistakes?

Yes — any AI can occasionally get something wrong. That's exactly why the process includes guardrails: the AI is trained only on your business information, it's given clear boundaries on what it should and shouldn't answer, and we test it together before launch. You're responsible for reviewing customer-facing outputs, and whenever you spot something you'd phrase differently, we tune it. This is also covered in our Terms & Conditions.

Who owns my website, my content and my customer data?

You do. Your website, your content and your customer information belong to you — before, during and after working with us. We configure AI tools for your business; we don't claim ownership of your site or your data, and we never sell personal data.

How is my customers' information handled?

Carefully, and only for the job you hired it to do — answering questions and getting inquiries to you. The basics are in our Privacy Policy: what information is collected, what it's used for, and how to reach us about it at support@artificial-intelligencenow.com. If a customer asks your AI something it shouldn't handle, it's set up to hand off rather than improvise.
